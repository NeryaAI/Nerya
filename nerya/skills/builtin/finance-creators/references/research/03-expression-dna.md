# Expression DNA

Research cutoff and access date: **2026-07-27**.

This is a small, purposive sample of official profiles, posts, and publication
formats. It supports recognizable delivery constraints, not exact statistical
claims about lifetime vocabulary or sentence length. The runtime Skill should
borrow useful information structure without first-person impersonation,
manufactured certainty, engagement bait, or catchphrase overuse.

## Serenity (`@aleabitoreddit`)

### Observable expression pattern

- **Ticker-dense technical prose.** Posts move quickly among named suppliers,
  products, architectures, capacity constraints, and end markets. The CPO post
  uses industry nouns such as pluggables, NPO, CPO, CW lasers, qualification,
  capacity, raw materials, and TAM inside one causal argument. [FE-SER-03]
- **Long-form compression with parentheses.** A single post can hold a source
  reference, qualification, counterargument, analogy, possible M&A path, and a
  final `TLDR`. Parentheses and plus signs carry side conditions instead of
  formal footnotes. [FE-SER-03]
- **Variant claim first.** Openings such as `Not exactly` or `There you have it`
  announce disagreement or confirmation before the evidence path. Tickers and
  the operational mechanism follow immediately. [FE-SER-02, FE-SER-03]
- **Questions make strategy concrete.** The Waymo reply asks the reader to take
  Uber's position and reason about who owns the user funnel. This turns an
  abstract channel-conflict thesis into a short decision thought experiment.
  [FE-SER-02]
- **Confidence and caveat coexist.** The Waymo reply uses maximal confidence,
  while the AMD channel-check post explicitly separates informal commentary
  from official company or partner guidance. [FE-SER-02, FE-SER-04]
- **Occasional affinity humor.** A disclosed AMD gift is followed by a playful
  declaration of affection. Treat that as entertainment and conflict context,
  not analytical evidence. [FE-SER-05]

### Runtime voice constraints

Lead with the variant thesis, then name the product, supplier, customer,
bottleneck, and commercialization consequence. Use technical language only
when defined by the evidence ledger. Preserve conditional words such as
`likely`, `seems`, and `feels` when the source is a channel check or inference.
Do not reproduce maximal certainty unless independently verified evidence
supports it, and never imitate personal gift jokes as investment conviction.

**Dimension strength: medium.** The public sample is distinctive but covers
roughly one year and is concentrated on X; it cannot establish a stable voice
across full market cycles, losses, or private deliberation.

## Unusual Whales (`@unusual_whales`)

### Observable expression pattern

- **News-desk fragments.** General-interest posts usually state one statistic
  or claim in one short sentence, ending with a compact attribution such as
  `per NYT` or `per WaPo`. [FE-UW-03, FE-UW-04]
- **Urgency labels.** Product launches and breaking developments often start
  with an all-caps label such as `BREAKING`, before supplying the datum and a
  link. [FE-UW-02]
- **Numbers before explanation.** The feed foregrounds percentages, dollars,
  tickers, timing, or a named anomaly; causal interpretation may be absent.
  This is efficient discovery packaging but incomplete analysis.
- **Editorial and commercial voices interleave.** The same feed moves from
  attributed news to API or tool promotion with direct calls to action such as
  checking a free trial or new market map. [FE-UW-01, FE-UW-02, FE-UW-05]
- **Brand voice, not singular personality.** Volume, topic breadth, team
  education, and product marketing make it unsafe to infer one person's humor,
  risk tolerance, or portfolio from the account.

### Runtime voice constraints

Use a compact alert header, then contract or disclosure details, attribution,
and the ambiguity checklist. Keep `per source` attribution but expand the
underlying filing or data source. Do not use `BREAKING`, outrage, or a product
CTA as a confidence signal. Avoid first-person voice because the account is a
platform and editorial brand.

**Dimension strength: high for brand packaging, low for individual voice.**
The feed is highly recognizable, but recognizability must not be confused with
a stable founder persona or validated signal quality.

## The Kobeissi Letter (`@KobeissiLetter`)

### Observable expression pattern

- **Headline-first macro compression.** Posts commonly open with `BREAKING`,
  `Shocking stat of the day`, or `Key Events This Week`, then state the largest
  number or event before the causal narrative. [FE-KOB-03, FE-KOB-04,
  FE-KOB-05]
- **Record and ratio framing.** The feed emphasizes records, multiples, changes
  from a prior period, and deviations from a long-run average. Capitalization
  and words such as `QUADRUPLED` make the comparison visually salient.
  [FE-KOB-04]
- **Visible causal bridge.** A statistic is often followed by `This comes as`
  or a similar clause linking issuance, policy, geopolitical events, funding,
  or asset prices.
- **Calendar as numbered narrative.** Weekly event posts compress policy,
  releases, and earnings into an ordered list with dates and tickers. This is a
  useful risk map when consensus, revisions, and priced expectations are added.
  [FE-KOB-05]
- **Promotion uses the same compression.** The pinned performance announcement
  leads with reported returns and benchmark comparison; methodology and
  limitations live in the linked report rather than the social hook.
  [FE-KOB-02]

### Runtime voice constraints

Lead with the surprise and its denominator, then show the transmission path
across assets. Use lists for event calendars and compact numerical comparisons,
but add consensus, previous value, revisions, source date, and contrary data.
Reserve urgency labels for genuinely time-sensitive facts. Never convert
all-caps emphasis, record framing, or a self-reported result into analytical
confidence.

**Dimension strength: medium-high for editorial voice, weak for spontaneous
deliberation.** The X feed and weekly publication are consistent; no qualifying
long-form interview was found to show how the team revises under questioning.

## Cross-Creator Expression Guardrail

| Creator | Preserve | Remove or expand |
|---|---|---|
| Serenity | mechanism, product specificity, conditional transition map | unverified channel certainty, affinity humor as evidence |
| Unusual Whales | alert clarity, attribution, exact contract or filing detail | outrage, anonymous brand-as-person, product urgency |
| Kobeissi | numerical surprise, event calendar, cross-asset chain | record framing without denominator, revisions, or alternatives |
