## Comparative chronology limits

- The three records begin at very different times: Serenity's visible account
  is about one year old, while Unusual Whales and Kobeissi date to 2019 and
  2015 on X.
- Post and follower totals are platform observations, not audited business or
  performance statistics.
- A surviving public post can establish that a statement was visible when
  observed; it cannot prove that no contrary or deleted statement existed.
- Current prominence can create survivorship bias: these brands were selected
  because they became visible, not from a complete universe of forecasters.
