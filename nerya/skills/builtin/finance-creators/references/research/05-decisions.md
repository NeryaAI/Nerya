# Decisions and Observable Calls

**Research cutoff:** 2026-07-27  
**Scope:** Serenity (`@aleabitoreddit`), Unusual Whales
(`@unusual_whales`), and The Kobeissi Letter (`@KobeissiLetter`).

## Evidence language

- **Direct statement:** words published by the creator or brand.
- **Observable action:** a dated publication, product release, or disclosed
  position change.
- **External report:** an identified third party's account; none is silently
  converted into a first-party fact.
- **Framework inference:** a cautious pattern inferred from more than one
  observation, never represented as private reasoning.

Public posts are a selected record. Deleted posts, private subscriber material,
unpublished exits, and account-level P&L are not observable here.

## Serenity

### Waymo, Uber, and Avride thesis

- **Direct statement:** in a quoted 2025-12-04 reply, Serenity said she was
  “100% sure” Uber would not partner with Waymo in every city and argued that
  routing users to Waymo could weaken Uber, while an owned neutral autonomy
  partner such as NBIS/Avride avoided that conflict. [FD-SER-02]
- **Direct statement / post-hoc update:** on 2026-07-24 she presented a reported
  Waymo-Uber split as confirmation of that earlier view. [FD-SER-02]
- **Boundary:** this is a business-structure thesis, not a timestamped security
  purchase with entry, exit, size, horizon, and total return. The follow-up is
  selected by the forecaster and the underlying FT report was not independently
  verified in this research pass.

### CPO and optical-networking calls

- **Direct statement:** on 2026-07-24 she rejected the simple conclusion that
  co-packaged optics would put AAOI “in trouble,” citing a podcast claim that
  pluggable optics could remain relevant for roughly another decade. [FD-SER-03]
- **Direct statement based on undisclosed sources:** on 2026-07-23 she said AMD
  networking-partner channel checks pointed toward CPO in future Helios
  generations and broader hyperscaler adoption. [FD-SER-04]
- **Boundary:** anonymous channel checks cannot be independently reproduced, and
  the public posts do not define measurable adoption deadlines or invalidation.

### Incentive and track-record boundary

- **Direct disclosure:** the profile says “no paid promos” but also says she may
  trade or hold discussed names. [FD-SER-01]
- **Observable relationship:** she disclosed that AMD sent limited merchandise
  and jokingly linked it to liking AMD and Lisa Su. [FD-SER-05] This is not proof
  of paid promotion, but it is a relationship readers should retain beside AMD
  commentary.
- No complete ledger of calls or misses was located. The absence of visible
  misses in this small sample is selection/survivorship risk, not evidence of a
  superior hit rate.

## Unusual Whales

### Congressional-trading research as the clearest observable decision

- **Observable action:** the brand published its 2021 congressional-trading
  report on 2022-01-10 after reviewing disclosures from 2020-12-01 through
  2021-12-22. It reported nearly $290 million in equity trades and concluded,
  under its approximation, that Congress beat SPY. [FD-UW-02]
- **Direct methodological limitation:** the report assumes closing prices,
  matches disclosed purchases and sales under stated rules, excludes unmatched
  sales, begins with data from 2020, and does not model options execution.
  Disclosure delays can make the underlying trades untradeable in real time.
- **Direct brand claim:** the About page says the report drew national attention
  and that six bills followed within six days. [FD-UW-01] Treat that causal
  language as the brand's claim; this pass did not independently establish that
  the report caused the bills.

### Product rather than advisory posture

- **Observable action:** by 2026 the platform offered options flow, dark-pool
  data, screeners, alerts, political-trading data, prediction-market data, and
  an API; a 2026-07-01 pinned post announced an API free trial. [FD-UW-04]
- **Direct boundary:** its Terms say content is not investment advice, data may
  be delayed or inaccurate, and employees may not trade or hold an alert, flow,
  ticker, or call-out. [FD-UW-03] Enforcement is not independently audited.
- **Framework inference:** the defensible persona is a signal-discovery and
  disclosure-audit lens, not a guru portfolio. Options flow is evidence of
  activity, not proof of direction, intent, or future return.

### Commercial incentives and missing misses

Unusual Whales sells subscriptions and API access and solicits advertising and
data partnerships. [FD-UW-03, FD-UW-05] Its public feed mixes breaking news,
data, and product promotion. No complete, timestamped call-and-return ledger was
found, so individual viral alerts cannot be scored as an audited track record.

## The Kobeissi Letter

### 2025 self-reported call ledger

- **Direct report:** the 2025 annual report claims a +30.66% aggregate return,
  versus +16.39% for the S&P 500, and +516.34% from 2020-2025 versus +111.88%
  for the index. [FD-KOB-01]
- **Observable reported calls:** examples include short S&P 500 on 2025-04-02
  to 04-03 (+2.72%), long S&P on 04-04 to 04-07 (-5.33%), then short on 04-07
  to 04-08 (+0.82%). The sequence shows both tactical reversal and a material
  miss; it should not be rewritten as an uninterrupted tariff-crash call.
- Reported section results were equities +21.77%, WTI crude +32.11%, natural gas
  +90.21%, gold +26.46%, and bonds -17.27%. The bond loss is an important
  counterexample to promotional summaries. Natural-gas losses included a
  -13.70% short and a -12.76% short alongside large gains. [FD-KOB-01]

### Computation and post-hoc risks

- **Direct methodology:** setup returns are sequentially compounded within
  each of five sections, then the section returns are equally weighted at 20%,
  assuming full reinvestment and no leverage. Options calls are excluded
  because subscribers choose their own exits. [FD-KOB-01]
- **Limitations:** the report is first-party and was not independently audited.
  It does not demonstrate broker fills or a live account, and its methodology
  does not state transaction costs, taxes, bid/ask slippage, or whether every
  contemporaneous subscriber setup appears unchanged in the year-end ledger.
  The dated report archive improves inspectability but is not a substitute for
  immutable, pre-trade records. [FD-KOB-02]

### Incentives

The brand sells recurring subscriptions, advertises an annual-plan discount,
and uses performance prominently on its homepage. [FD-KOB-03] Its PDF and legal
page state that material is educational, not investment advice, and that past
performance does not predict future results. [FD-KOB-01, FD-KOB-05] Commercial
success and follower count must not be used as evidence of investment skill.

## Cross-creator conclusion

- Serenity offers concentrated, technically detailed theses but the shortest
  and least auditable record.
- Unusual Whales primarily supplies data and disclosure infrastructure; its
  outputs require a separate thesis, execution plan, and risk model.
- Kobeissi supplies the clearest call ledger, including misses, but the ledger
  remains self-reported rather than an audited portfolio.
