# Source-Backed Finance Creator Lenses

Use these as distinct research modes. Do not impersonate, endorse, or invent a
quotation for a creator. Source IDs support a creator's published method or a
past statement. Applying that method to a new asset must be labelled
`framework inference`.

## Lens Router

| Decision | Primary lens | Useful challenger |
|---|---|---|
| AI infrastructure, semiconductors, networking, or supplier transitions | Serenity | Kobeissi for macro timing |
| Options activity, gamma exposure, dark pools, or public-official trading | Unusual Whales | Kobeissi for the event path |
| Macro releases, policy, commodities, or cross-asset stress | The Kobeissi Letter | Serenity for industry evidence |

Use one lens by default. Audience size is not a selection criterion.

## Serenity: Supply-Chain Signal Lens

**Distinctive question:** Which product transition, supplier relationship,
bottleneck, or distribution conflict changes the earnings path before it is
obvious in aggregate data?

### Core models

1. **Map the physical chain.** Trace component, system, customer, qualification,
   capacity, and end demand instead of treating "AI" as one undifferentiated
   trade. Her public profile explicitly centers AI and semiconductor supply
   chains. [CL-SER-01]
2. **Commercialization stage beats theme labels.** Separate a roadmap from
   sampling, qualification, volume shipment, and customer adoption. Her CPO
   discussion treats pluggables and co-packaged optics as a transition with
   timing and supplier-specific paths, not an instant substitution. [CL-SER-03]
3. **Channel conflict is a valuation variable.** Ask whether a partner is also
   building the product, distribution, or customer relationship that can make
   the intermediary dispensable. Her Waymo-Uber analysis uses this structure.
   [CL-SER-02]
4. **Channel checks are leads, not audited facts.** Named executives, public
   material, partner conversations, and anonymous industry contacts have
   different evidence grades. Her AMD networking post demonstrates the speed
   and specificity of this method, while also exposing its reproducibility
   limit. [CL-SER-04]

### Decision heuristics

- Draw supplier, customer, substitute, bottleneck, and distribution maps before
  discussing a ticker.
- Label every channel datum as public, named private, anonymous private, or
  inference; require independent confirmation for portfolio implications.
- Ask what must ship, qualify, or scale in the next two product generations.
- Treat gifts, access, disclosed holdings, and company interaction as context,
  not automatic disqualification or validation. [CL-SER-01, CL-SER-05]

### Reasoning voice

Use ticker-dense, concrete, reply-aware analysis. Lead with the variant claim,
name the supplier or product mechanism, then state the commercialization or
distribution consequence. Preserve her high-conviction cadence only as style;
calibrate the conclusion to the evidence actually verified.

### Failure boundaries and tensions

- The account is pseudonymous, joined X in July 2025, and has a short public
  record relative to a full industry cycle. [CL-SER-01]
- Private channel checks may be selective, stale, misunderstood, or impossible
  for another analyst to reproduce.
- The profile says no paid promotions and says positions may be held or traded;
  company access or gifts can still create appearance and selection effects.
  [CL-SER-01, CL-SER-05]
- Speed and specificity are useful for discovery but can outrun auditability.

## Unusual Whales: Flow and Disclosure Anomaly Lens

**Distinctive question:** What unusual positioning or delayed public record is
worth investigating, and which benign market-structure explanation must be
eliminated before inferring intent?

### Core models

1. **Anomaly starts the investigation.** Unusual size, premium, tenor, volume,
   open interest, or timing creates a query; it does not identify the trader,
   opening versus closing status, hedge, spread, or directional intent.
2. **Positioning is structural.** Read options flow beside liquidity, strike,
   expiry, event risk, and market-maker gamma exposure. The product itself
   foregrounds real-time flow and GEX rather than a standalone trade alert.
   [CL-UW-01, CL-UW-02]
3. **Public disclosures are lagged datasets.** Congressional transactions can
   reveal themes and conflicts, but reported ranges, ownership, filing delay,
   amendments, and missing economic context limit inference. The annual report
   is a screening surface, not proof of causality or privileged timing.
   [CL-UW-03]
4. **Retail access changes the search space.** Packaging institutional-style
   datasets can broaden hypothesis generation, while the platform's paid tools
   and alerts create a commercial incentive toward frequent engagement.
   [CL-UW-01, CL-UW-02, CL-UW-04]

### Decision heuristics

- Reconstruct the contract, premium, strike, expiry, volume, open interest,
  underlying liquidity, and nearby catalyst before interpreting a print.
- Test opening, closing, hedge, spread, and dealer-inventory explanations.
- For an official transaction, open the original filing and record transaction
  date, filing date, owner, value range, amendment status, and reporting lag.
- Use repeated, independently supported patterns; reject single-print certainty.

### Reasoning voice

Use fast alert structure, numbers, tickers, and a clear anomaly. Remove meme or
outrage packaging from the evidence ledger. Because this is a high-volume brand
account and data platform, model an editorial voice rather than a single human
personality.

### Failure boundaries and tensions

- Options flow alone does not reveal beneficial owner, full position, hedge,
  or directional intent.
- Public-official disclosures can be delayed, amended, range-valued, or tied to
  a spouse or dependent; headline precision can exceed source precision.
- The account markets subscriptions, alerts, API access, and trading tools.
  Commercial utility and editorial urgency must be labelled separately.
  [CL-UW-01, CL-UW-02, CL-UW-04]
- Democratizing data can improve access while encouraging anomaly chasing and
  false pattern confidence.

## The Kobeissi Letter: Macro Event-Compression Lens

**Distinctive question:** What changed relative to expectations, how does the
surprise transmit across assets, and which confirmation or reversal signal
matters next?

### Core models

1. **Surprise, not level, drives the first move.** Compare a release with
   consensus, the prior value, and revisions before narrating the reaction.
2. **Cross-asset confirmation.** Map the claimed shock through rates, dollar,
   equities, credit, commodities, and volatility; divergence is evidence that
   the simple narrative may be incomplete.
3. **Calendar creates a risk map.** Compress the coming week's policy,
   inflation, labor, earnings, and issuance events into catalysts, then define
   what is priced and what remains asymmetric.
4. **Blend fundamental and technical context.** The publisher describes its
   weekly letter as fundamental and technical analysis across the S&P 500,
   energy, gold, bonds, and options, with a separate Chart of the Week.
   [CL-KOB-02, CL-KOB-03]

### Decision heuristics

- State actual, consensus, previous, and revisions for every macro release.
- Separate the immediate price reaction from the durable regime implication.
- Require confirmation across at least two economically linked markets.
- Mark factual headline, interpretation, tactical call, and subscription
  marketing as separate objects.

### Reasoning voice

Use headline-first, compressed, number-rich market narration with a visible
causal chain. Preserve urgency only when an event is genuinely time-sensitive.
Expand caveats that a social post compresses; do not import engagement language
as analytical confidence.

### Failure boundaries and tensions

- Compression can omit base rates, denominators, revisions, positioning, and
  alternative causal explanations.
- The publication sells premium analysis and publishes self-described
  performance material; evaluate methodology and selection rules separately.
  [CL-KOB-02, CL-KOB-03]
- Its legal disclosure says affiliates may advise clients and trade referenced
  investments, including in ways that differ from published views. [CL-KOB-04]
- News-like authority, tactical opinion, affiliate activity, and subscription
  marketing can coexist; labels must prevent them from blending.

## Disagreement Matrix

| Tension | Route by |
|---|---|
| Serenity's private industry evidence vs. Unusual Whales' observable flow | Evidence grade, horizon, and reproducibility |
| Company transition thesis vs. Kobeissi macro reaction | Product-cycle horizon and financing or demand sensitivity |
| Options anomaly vs. macro catalyst | Contract structure, event timing, and alternative hedging explanations |

Do not vote. Return the governing assumption, the strongest contrary evidence,
and the observation that would resolve the disagreement.

## Citation Rules

- Cite at least one source ID for every named lens used.
- Quote only after verifying exact words in the primary source. Otherwise
  paraphrase and label the new application `framework inference`.
- Attach an `as_of` date and separate source ID to current market facts. The
  register below supports framework fidelity, not today's market state.
- Load the relevant file under `research/` for deeper provenance and criticism.
