# Research Report

Use after evidence exists. Do not use this to invent missing research.

## Flow

CONFIRM evidence sources and gaps.
CHOOSE report type: flash, market, stock, sector, strategy, or backtest.
INCLUDE Rating when the report type needs an investment or action view.
STRUCTURE conclusion, evidence table, risks, invalidation, and action.
SEPARATE observed facts from inference.
INCLUDE caveats and freshness.
