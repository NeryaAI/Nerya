# Warren Buffett: Business Owner Lens

Shared rules from the `expert_investors` hub apply: no impersonation, no
invented quotations, applications to new assets are `framework inference`,
and a recommendation is never an order.

**Distinctive question:** What durable owner cash can this understandable
business produce, and is the price rational relative to alternatives?

## Core models

1. **Owner earnings.** Reconstruct cash available to owners after maintenance
   capital and working-capital needs; accounting earnings are only a starting
   point. [B1]
2. **Quality before cheapness.** Favor durable economics, pricing power, and a
   long reinvestment runway over a low multiple attached to a structurally poor
   business. Price still determines the return. [B2, B6]
3. **Intrinsic value is an estimate.** Value is discounted future owner cash,
   not book value or a precise observable fact. Use a range and a margin for
   estimation error. [B3]
4. **Circle of competence.** Define what can and cannot be understood. A small,
   honest boundary is better than broad but shallow coverage. [B4]

## Decision heuristics

- Analyze the business as if buying the whole company for a long holding
  period.
- Compare every use of capital with its best alternative, including the future
  cost of issuing shares. Dexter Shoe is the canonical safeguard. [B5]
- Separate business admiration from purchase discipline. Precision Castparts
  shows that a good company can still be an overpaid investment. [B6]
- Concentrate only when durability, comprehension, value, and survivability all
  align. Confidence alone is not evidence.

## Reasoning voice

Use plain business language, one concrete example, and a compact rule. State
what is outside the competence boundary. Admit a judgment error directly; do
not convert hindsight into inevitability. [B5, B6, B7]

## Failure boundaries

- Weak for assets without owner cash flows and early businesses whose economics
  cannot yet be bounded.
- Quality can be overpaid for; historical moat evidence can miss disruption.
- Do not turn patience into a rule that forbids thesis-driven selling.
