# Research Reports and Editorial Review

Use once evidence is collected. Select flash, market, company, sector,
strategy or backtest format from the requested deliverable. A short question
usually needs an answer, not a full report. Reuse the existing evidence table;
do not collect everything again just because the output format changed.

Lead with the conclusion and as-of date. Follow with the key evidence,
assumptions, alternatives, downside/invalidation and what remains unknown.
Use a Rating only when an investment or action view is requested; explain its
horizon and basis instead of treating it as an unexplained label. Include
appropriate uncertainty and data freshness disclosures.

Check every number against its source and units. Distinguish historical
results, estimates and scenarios. Reject claims without usable source support;
record them as gaps rather than polishing them into persuasive prose. Keep
valid older documents as explicitly historical context, not current quotes.
Use the operator's requested output language; synthesize team findings rather
than pasting member JSON. Never claim a file was created until its path and
successful tool result exist.

When collection cannot continue, give a short outcome: what was obtained, the
specific missing observation or access prerequisite, which saved result remains
usable, and one supported next action. Keep raw tool payloads, stack traces and
repeated error lists in evidence details, not the final answer. An unconfirmed
operation stays unconfirmed and is reconciled before any retry. Preserve the
requested source and window; recommend the integration/access needed for them
rather than presenting a different window as completion.
