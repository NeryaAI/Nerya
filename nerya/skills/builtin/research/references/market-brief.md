# Market, Stock and Token Brief

Use for a bounded professional research view, not an exhaustive data dump.
Confirm the asset, venue, horizon and question from the assignment. Get one
market summary for price structure and one complete `research_run` for the
needed business/token, macro, sentiment, flow and catalyst evidence.

After those return, build one thesis with supporting and disconfirming facts.
For stocks separate financial statements, estimates and valuation assumptions;
for tokens distinguish usage, protocol revenue, unlocks and ownership risks.
Use `markets` for source/venue ambiguity and `equity_research` or
`dcf_valuation` only when detailed fundamentals or valuation is required.
Do not reload multiple overlapping research playbooks for the same evidence.

A useful answer includes a dated conclusion, three to five material findings,
risks, an invalidation condition and calibrated confidence. If current data
failed, lead with the failure and distinguish any older evidence. No research
rating is an authorization to trade. Do not conceal source gaps with mock data.
