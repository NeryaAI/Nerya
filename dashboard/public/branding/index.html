Evolutionary Brain

Six logo directions for Nerya, the self-evolving trading runtime. Each variant carries the same conceptual nucleus — a biological neural network that grows, connects, and iterates — expressed with a different visual grammar: synapses, spirals, dendrites, ridges, double helix, and a phase loop. Display surface uses a high-saturation paper-texture backdrop with a violet ↔ cyan aurora bleed, mirroring the dashboard's runtime aesthetic.

01 · Synapse N
N as a neural circuit
The N letterform redrawn as three excitable axons with synaptic relay points. The diagonal carries a downstream cascade of small fluid dots — packets of information traveling from one neuron to the next.
weight 2.6 · stroke + nodes
02 · Iteration Spiral
The evolution loop
A 270° gradient arc closes back on itself with an open mouth — the hand-off of one generation to the next. Concentric dotted rings represent feedback iterations. The five satellite nodes echo the five pillars of the runtime: skill · plan · act · memory · evolve.
arc + concentric · 5 satellites
03 · Dendrite Tree
A cell body, branching outward
Most literal of the six — a single neuron with axon, dendrites, and synaptic terminals. The center cell glows with a luminous nucleus. Reads as both biology and decision tree, which is how the agent actually thinks.
organic · 9 terminals
04 · N + Brain Fold
Letterform with cortical ridges
Solid violet N filled with horizontal cortical fold lines — the visual signature of a brain in cross-section. A single fluid orb hovers at the upper-right tracking position, calling out the "agent online" status pattern from the dashboard.
solid + ridges · clip-path
05 · Orbital Genome
DNA double helix
Two intertwined gradient strands cross at five base-pair rungs — encoding, replication, mutation. Speaks to evolution at the genome level, which fits a system that rewrites its own strategies and skills on every cycle.
double helix · 5 base pairs
06 · Phase Loop
Infinity with a pulse
A horizontal infinity / Möbius ribbon sweeps left-to-right with the cyan-violet gradient, then crosses through a glowing white pulse node at center. This is the "always-running, always-learning" loop signature, and the cleanest of the six at favicon size.
infinity · pulse node
