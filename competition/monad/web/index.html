03 Know what this assumes

A deliberately narrow stress test

Flat account. One hypothetical position. An adverse move in either direction. Two taker fees from the public market configuration and two slippage allowances you choose.

USD is a comparison unit. Capital is your assumption, not a wallet balance. “Within scenario budget” applies only to this calculation.

What the numbers leave out

Funding, liquidation, margin requirements, gas, actual fills, existing positions, and execution depth are excluded. The maximum notional is a mathematical budget ceiling, not an executable size recommendation.

Observations older than 120 seconds, or more than 10 seconds in the future, require review even if the loss fits the budget.

Show the shared-core calculation

Move loss = notional × adverse move ÷ 100.
Round-trip costs = notional × (2 × taker fee bps + 2 × assumed slippage bps) ÷ 10,000.
Stress loss = move loss + round-trip costs.
Loss budget = hypothetical capital × loss budget % ÷ 100.

Both sizes call the same scenario() in the shared core. Source age is recalculated while this page stays open.
